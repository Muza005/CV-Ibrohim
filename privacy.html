Privacy

Short version: this site does not track you and does not store anything you type.

What this site collects

Nothing on purpose. This is a static portfolio: there are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking scripts, and the fonts are served from this site rather than a third-party font service.

The contact form

When you press Send Message, your browser opens your own email app with the message filled in. Nothing is sent to or stored on this website: the email goes straight from your mailbox to mine, and you can review it before you press send. If you choose Open in Gmail, the message is opened in Gmail under Google's own privacy terms.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, it may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details in its logs in order to deliver and protect the site.
