How I work

A simple, predictable process that keeps business, frontend and backend aligned.

  1. step 01

    $ discover

    Understand the business process, the users and the constraints, and write them down as clear requirements.

  2. step 02

    $ spec

    Design the data model and the API contract first (OpenAPI) and agree on it with frontend, QA and stakeholders.

  3. step 03

    $ build

    Implement in small, independently structured modules with validation and consistent error handling.

  4. step 04

    $ secure

    Add authentication, RBAC, rate limits and audit logging, and test the edge cases, not just the happy path.

  5. step 05

    $ ship

    Deploy behind Nginx on Linux, document what was built, watch the logs and keep improving.
